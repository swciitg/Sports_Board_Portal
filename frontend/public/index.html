Sports Board, IIT Guwahati

The Sports Board, IIT Guwahati is the student body responsible for coordinating sporting activities across the Indian Institute of Technology Guwahati campus. It oversees the institute's sports clubs, organizes tournaments and events through the academic year, and publishes announcements for student athletes and the wider campus sporting community. This site lists every sports club on campus along with its activities and achievements, keeps an up to date calendar of events and tournaments, and shares official announcements from the board.
